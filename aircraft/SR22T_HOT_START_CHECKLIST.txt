CIRRUS SR22T G7+ HOT START CHECKLIST
N269PG | Continental TSIO-550-K
Based on: G7 AFM + COPA Community Knowledge

DEFINITION: "Hot Start" = Engine shutdown less than 15 minutes
If warm but longer ground time, use normal start with reduced priming

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRE-START SETUP (Engine Warm)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Flight Controls                                          FREE & CORRECT
□ Flight Instruments                                         CHECKED
□ Fuel Quantity                                            VERIFIED
□ CAS Messages                                             CHECK (clear before start)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HOT START SEQUENCE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FUEL & MIXTURE SETUP:
□ Fuel Pump                                                BOOST (ON)
□ Mixture Control                  FULL LEAN or ALMOST CLOSED
  * Critical: Cold = almost closed, warm = full lean
  * Community tip: Start lean, advance slowly during crank

ENGINE PREPARATION:
□ Power Lever                         1/4 to 3/4 FORWARD
  * Less is more: 1/4 open is often sufficient
  * If no start after 2 attempts, try 3/4 open
□ Engine Knob                                              BOTH
□ Propeller Area                                           CLEAR
□ Brakes                                                   HOLD

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STARTING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Starter                                                  ENGAGE
□ While Cranking                              SLOWLY ADVANCE MIXTURE
  * Advance mixture control smoothly toward RICH
  * Engine should fire as mixture reaches operating range
  * Stop advancing when engine catches

POST-CRANK / ENGINE FIRES:
□ Starter                                         RELEASE immediately
□ Power Lever                      RETARD to maintain ~1000 RPM
  * Don't let engine surge; keep it steady at idle
□ Monitor Oil Pressure               MUST RISE within 30 seconds
  * Cold weather: may take up to 60 seconds; shut down if no rise

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AFTER START (Taxi Prep)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Mixture Control                LEAN until RPM PEAKS, then leave it
  * This is peak-RPM lean for taxi, not full lean
  * Adjust as needed for rough running
□ ALT 1 & ALT 2 Switches                                    ON
□ Engine Parameters                                    MONITOR
□ Avionics Initialization                    ALL INITS COMPLETE
□ CAPS Pin                                          REMOVE & STOW
□ External Power (if used)                         DISCONNECT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FLOODED ENGINE RECOVERY (If Engine Won't Start)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Signs of flooding: Weak intermittent firing, black smoke from exhaust

□ Fuel Pump                                                  OFF
□ Let fuel drain from intake tubes              WAIT 5-10 seconds
□ Mixture Control                                     FULL LEAN
□ Power Lever                                       FULL FORWARD
□ Starter                                 CRANK several revolutions
  * 10-15 seconds of cranking
□ When engine fires:
  □ Starter                                          RELEASE
  □ Power Lever                                      RETARD
  □ Mixture Control                SLOWLY advance toward RICH
□ Fuel Pump                                                  ON
□ Stabilize at ~1000 RPM

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STARTUP TROUBLESHOOTING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SYMPTOMS: Won't crank at all
→ Verify: Battery charge, fuel pump ON, mixture not CUTOFF

SYMPTOMS: Sluggish cranking
→ Community fix: Check starter condition
  * Stock Cirrus starter is weak; Tempest starter much better
  * Weak starter + hot day = extended crank time = battery drain

SYMPTOMS: Engine cranks but won't fire
→ Verify: Engine Knob BOTH (not one mag)
→ Try: Fuel Pump to HIGH BOOST/PRIME for 2 seconds (AFM note)
→ Try: Power Lever to 3/4 open (lean start might be too lean)

SYMPTOMS: Rough running after start
→ Verify: Oil pressure rising normally
→ Check: Mixture control position (may need slight adjustment)
→ Normal: Some rough running resolves as engine warms

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CRITICAL NOTES (AFM + COPA Consensus)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. WARM ENGINE = NO PRIME NEEDED
   If engine warm, do NOT hold fuel pump to HIGH BOOST
   Normal BOOST is sufficient

2. MIXTURE IS THE KEY
   Community pilots emphasize: start lean, advance slowly while
   cranking. Do not advance mixture before cranking or too fast.

3. POWER LEVER (THROTTLE) MANAGEMENT
   • Less throttle is often better than full forward
   • If engine fires and power is high, pull back immediately
   • Target idle speed after start: 1000 RPM

4. STARTER DUTY CYCLES
   AFM limit: 10 seconds crank / 20 seconds cool between attempts
   Protects battery and starter

5. OIL PRESSURE IS YOUR CHECK
   Engine must show rising oil pressure within 30 sec of start
   If no pressure rise: SHUT DOWN and troubleshoot

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ENGINE SHUTDOWN (for reference)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Power Lever                                               IDLE
□ Engine Knob                                             CYCLE
  * Verify engine hesitates as knob passes "OFF" position
  * If no hesitation: one or both mags not grounded (ERROR)
□ Mixture Control                                        CUTOFF
□ All Switches (ALT 1, ALT 2, STRB, etc.)                 OFF
□ Engine Knob                                              OFF
□ CAPS Pin                                              REPLACE
□ Aircraft                                 SECURE (chocks/tie-downs)
